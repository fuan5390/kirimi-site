Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Who we are

KIRIMI Channel makes short animated English lessons for young children. This policy covers the channel's private publishing tool, which is used only by the channel owner.

What we collect

  • When the channel owner connects their TikTok account, the tool receives an access token that lets it upload the channel's own videos. The token is stored only on the owner's own computer.
  • We do not collect, store or sell personal information about viewers or visitors, and this website has no forms, accounts, analytics or cookies.
  • We do not knowingly collect information from children. Our videos are for children to watch with their parents, but we collect no data from them.

How we use it

The access token is used only to upload the channel's own videos to the channel's own account. It is never shared with third parties.

Your choices

The owner can revoke the tool's access at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services.
